Solutions for Institutes

How we solve everyday administrative and teaching nightmares.

1. Content & Course Delivery

Problem: Scattered Study Materials

Teachers send YouTube links on WhatsApp, email PDF notes separately, and conduct quizzes elsewhere. Students constantly lose track of where to study.

Solution: The Master Course Builder

We provide a centralized Course Builder. Within a single 'Module', a teacher can embed a Video Lecture, attach downloadable PDF Notes, and append a Quiz. It provides a linear, organized learning path for the student.

2. Conducting Assessments

Problem: Time-Consuming Test Creation

Typing out hundreds of questions for mock tests takes days. Managing Hindi and English versions doubles the workload. Checking answers manually is impossible at scale.

Solution: Bilingual Bulk Uploads & Auto-Grading

Institutes can upload a single CSV file to instantly import thousands of questions in both English and Hindi simultaneously. Our Assessment Engine lets you build Mock Tests in seconds, and auto-evaluates student answers instantly.

3. Administration & HR

Problem: Security & Staff Chaos

Giving all staff the master admin password is a huge security risk. Also, onboarding 500 offline students to an online app manually takes weeks.

Solution: RBAC & Bulk Onboarding

RBAC (Role-Based Access): Create restricted accounts for Teachers (only teach) and Operators (only upload).
Bulk Onboarding: Upload 500 students via a single CSV file. The system instantly generates digital ID cards for all of them.

4. Student Tracking

Problem: Paper Attendance Registers

Tracking daily attendance on paper is inefficient and makes it impossible to provide quick reports to concerned parents.

Solution: Digital Attendance System

Select a date and a batch. The roster appears, and you simply click Present/Absent/Late. The system calculates percentages automatically, allowing you to instantly identify drop-outs or disengaged students.

Solutions for Students

Creating an environment built for focus, accuracy, and engagement.

1. Test Anxiety & Interface

Problem: Poor Testing Interfaces

Taking tests on standard websites doesn't prepare students for actual Government/Competitive exams. The UI is usually just a long scrolling page of text.

Solution: Pro Exam Simulation UI

Our Mock Test player replicates real-world testing environments. It features a sticky Question Palette grid showing exactly what is Answered vs Unanswered. It enforces strict countdown timers and handles negative marking accurately, training students for the real deal.

2. Clearing Doubts

Problem: Unanswered Questions

If a student doesn't understand a concept in a recorded video, they have no easy way to ask the teacher without losing context or spamming WhatsApp groups.

Solution: Integrated Course Q&A Forums

Directly beneath every course, there is a dedicated Q&A forum. Students post doubts, teachers reply, and common questions are "Pinned" to the top. The knowledge stays organized and attached to the course itself.

3. Staying Organized

Problem: "Where did I leave off?"

Students struggle to remember which video they watched last, or when their next live class is scheduled.

Solution: The Student Dashboard

The dashboard tracks progress automatically. Students see a "Resume Learning" button for exactly where they left off. Upcoming Live Classes have a countdown timer right on the home page so they never miss a session.

Monetization & Growth

Problem: Low Sales & Invisible Websites

Most LMS platforms are private. Google cannot index the courses, meaning zero organic search traffic. Plus, institutes miss out on passive ad revenue.

Solution: SEO & Smart AdSense

SEO Landing Pages: Every course automatically generates a stunning public page with meta-tags and schemas designed to rank on Google. Students can request enrollment directly from these pages.

Zero-Code AdSense: Super Admins can paste raw Google AdSense code into the dashboard. Abhyo dynamically injects ad banners across the site, creating a passive revenue stream without needing developers.
